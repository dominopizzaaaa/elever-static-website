Élever — to raise, to elevate.

Élever (verb, French) — to build or raise; to bring something to a higher position. We named the academy after the thing we are actually trying to do.

Our story

Why Élever exists

Élever Badminton was founded on a simple frustration: in Singapore, how good a player becomes depends far too much on which hall they happen to live near and which coach they happen to meet. Talent is everywhere. Structure is not.

So we built the pathway we wished had existed — four progressive stages, each with a defined set of skills, a clear entry point and a stated way to move up. A parent should be able to see exactly where their child is and what comes next. A player should never be stuck in the same session for three years without knowing why.

That pathway now runs from holiday camps for complete beginners through to a performance squad preparing for national age-group competition, taught by coaches who work to the same curriculum wherever you train with us.

How we coach

Our approach

Technique before power. A stroke built properly at eight still works at eighteen. One built for short-term results has to be unlearned, and unlearning is far harder than learning.

Small groups, real coaching. A player who spends the session in a queue is not being coached. We cap group sizes so every player is actually seen and corrected.

One curriculum, every venue. What gets taught in Essentials is the same whether you train in Jurong East or Sengkang. Our Technical Director owns it, and our coaches are trained to it.

Enjoyment is not the opposite of rigour. Particularly with beginners — a child who enjoys the session repeats the movement without being asked.

Élever operates as a registered coaching provider with appropriate insurance in place for our sessions.
