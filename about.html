Our story

Enhancing the badminton experience for all.

Founded in June 2023 by Loh Kean Hean and Eng Chin An, Élever Badminton aims to enhance the badminton experience for all. From players taking their first swings to seasoned elites looking to maximise their fullest potential, our team is committed to building strong foundations and guiding players through a structured pathway that supports their individual aspirations. Embark on this exciting journey of growth with us and elevate your game.
