Singapore Badminton Hub

Singapore badminton court guide: Use this hub to compare badminton courts for rent in Singapore, including public ActiveSG halls, private badminton halls, community club courts and family-friendly places to play in Central, North, North-East, East and West Singapore.

Addresses are compiled from Google Maps and the official SportSG facilities dataset. ActiveSG public halls can also be booked in the MyActiveSG app. Community Club (CC) courts are booked on OnePA. Spotted a mistake or a missing hall? Tell us.

01

Private halls

Air-conditioned courts you rent by the hour, usually through each hall's own website or app. Best for a guaranteed slot with no balloting. Prices vary — roughly S$20–40 per hour per court.

  • Pick a hall on the tab.
  • Open its booking link and choose a date and time.
  • Pay online to confirm — you're set.
02

ActiveSG public halls

The most affordable way to play — from about S$3.50–7.40 per hour. Book at activesg.gov.sg or in the MyActiveSG app.

  • Log in with Singpass, then choose Book a Facility → Badminton.
  • Search by postal code or venue. Each slot is 1 hour (up to 2 per day).
  • Peak hours (weekdays after 6pm, weekends and public holidays) use a ballot that opens about 14 days ahead. Off-peak is first-come, released about 13 days ahead at 12pm.
03

Community Club (CC) courts

Run by the People's Association across the island. Prices vary by CC, roughly S$5–7 per hour. Book on OnePA.

  • Log in with Singpass, then choose Facilities → Book a Facility.
  • Select Badminton Court, then your region, date and time.
  • New slots open daily at 10pm, up to 15 days ahead.

Casual & social play

Most social games form up on community platforms. Try these to find a session near you and at your level:

  • Racket Ratings Clubs — rated players and ladders
  • Meetup — Singapore badminton
  • Facebook and Telegram “kaki” groups (search “badminton Singapore”)
  • Your neighbourhood CC — many run drop-in social sessions

Ready to compete?

Once you have a rating, Racket Ratings lists open tournaments you can enter — and lets your own group create one. A low-pressure way into competitive play.
