The four E's

Development pathways

Two ways to train

Group class or private class

Group Class

Train, learn, and progress together. Our Group Classes maintain a maximum 1:6 coach-to-player ratio and are organised according to our development pathway: Essentials, Emergence, and Elite. Players train alongside others at a similar level, creating a focused environment for structured learning, consistent development, and quality on-court experience.

See days, times and venues

Private Class

A more personalised approach to your game. Available in 1-to-1 or 1-to-2 formats, our Private Classes provide targeted coaching based on each player's individual needs and goals. With greater attention to specific areas of development, training can be tailored to refine technique, address weaknesses, and maximise progress.

  • Video analysis
  • Competition preparation
