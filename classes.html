The four E's

Development pathways

Four progressive stages. Each one has a clear entry point, a defined set of skills, and a stated way to move up — so you always know where a player is and what comes next.

Two ways to train

Regular classes or private coaching

Regular classes

Weekly group coaching at a fixed venue, day and time, grouped by level rather than age alone. This is how most Élever players train — the consistency of a squad, the accountability of a group, and a coach who sees the same players every week.

  • Fixed weekly slot at a venue near you
  • Grouped by playing level — Essentials or Emergence
  • Small groups so every player gets coached
  • A clear route into the next pathway stage
See days, times and venues

Private classes

One-to-one or small-group coaching arranged around your goal and your schedule. Best for players working on something specific — a technical fix, competition preparation, or a fast start for a beginner who would rather not learn in a group.

  • One-to-one, pairs or a small family group
  • Your choice of venue, day and time
  • Programme built around a specific goal
  • Available to juniors and adults
