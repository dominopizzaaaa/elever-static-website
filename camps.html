Holiday camps where badminton starts.

Our holiday badminton camps in Singapore turn a school break into a first real taste of the sport. They are built for players with little or no experience — game-based, high-energy, and designed so that children finish the week asking when the next one is.

What we do at an Exploration camp

Exploration is the first stage of the Élever pathway, and a holiday camp is the easiest way into it. Over four mornings we build the things a beginner actually needs — coordination, movement, racket familiarity and the confidence to keep a rally going.

Almost none of it looks like drilling. Sessions are game-based, because a child who is enjoying themselves repeats a movement fifty times without noticing, and a child who is bored does not repeat it at all.

Groups are small and split by age and ability, so a complete beginner is never stuck in a queue behind someone who has played for two years. We lend rackets to anyone who does not have one.
