05 — Singapore Badminton Hub

Everything badminton, one place.

There's no single place to find where to play in Singapore — so we built one. Browse every hall, learn how to book a court, and find a group to hit with. Not just an academy — the home of badminton in SG.

Addresses compiled from Google Maps & the official SportSG facilities dataset. ActiveSG public halls are also bookable via the MyActiveSG app. Community Club (CC) courts can be booked at OnePA. Spot an error or a missing hall? Let us know.

01

Private halls

Air-conditioned courts you rent by the hour, usually through each hall's own booking site or app. Great for a guaranteed slot with no balloting. Prices vary — expect roughly S$20–40/hr per court.

  • Pick a hall from the tab.
  • Open its booking link and choose a date & time.
  • Pay online to confirm — done.
02

ActiveSG public halls

The cheapest way to play — from about S$3.50–7.40/hr. Book on activesg.gov.sg or the MyActiveSG app.

  • Log in with Singpass → Book a Facility → Badminton.
  • Search by postal code or venue; each slot is 1 hour (max 2/day).
  • Peak (weekday after 6pm, weekends & PH) is balloted — the window opens ~14 days ahead. Off-peak is first-come, released ~13 days ahead at 12pm.
03

Community Club (CC) courts

Run by the People's Association across the island. Prices vary per CC, roughly S$5–7/hr. Book at OnePA.

  • Log in with Singpass → Facilities → Book a Facility.
  • Choose Badminton Court, region, date & time.
  • New slots open daily at 10pm, up to 15 days in advance.
