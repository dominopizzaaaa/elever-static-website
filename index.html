What is Élever

Élever (verb, French) — to build or raise; to bring something to a higher position.

We named the academy after the thing we are actually trying to do. Élever Badminton is a Singapore coaching academy built around a single structured pathway — four progressive stages that take a player from a first swing in a holiday camp all the way to competitive play.

We also run badminton as a service for other organisations: carnivals, clinics and competitions for companies, schools and community groups across the island.
