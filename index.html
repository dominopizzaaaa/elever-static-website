01 — About Élever

More than a sport.
A pathway to greatness.

Élever (verb) — to build or raise; bring something to a higher position.

Excellence is a journey — and at Élever Badminton it follows a meticulously structured pathway built on progressive pillars. From your very first swing to elite-level competition, our coaches provide the expert mentorship and supportive community every athlete needs to build confidence and master the game.

We believe every player deserves world-class guidance, wherever they are in their journey. That belief drives everything we do — on and off the court.

03 — What We Do

Four ways to rise.

01

Camps

Our Holiday Exploration Camps turn every school break into a badminton adventure. Game-based learning for players with little to no experience — building foundations and physical development while leaving them eager to keep playing.

02

Classes

A structured pathway built on four progressive pillars. From your very first swing to elite competition, our classes provide expert mentorship and a supportive community to build confidence and master the game.

03

Clinics

Dynamic, community-driven training experiences — specialised, short-term workshops. Players dive deep into mechanics, footwork and strategy in a high-energy, immersive environment tailored to specific age groups and every skill level.

04

Carnivals

Community outreach combining clinics, fun games and community activities. By bringing the sport into selected communities, we make badminton accessible to all — celebrating health, connection and the joy of play.

05 — The 2026 Season

Weekly from the world tour.

← drag the timeline →

11–13 Jan 2026 Malaysia Open · Super 1000

An & Kunlavut open the season with Super 1000 crowns

An Se-young completed a Malaysia Open three-peat in women's singles while Kunlavut Vitidsarn claimed his maiden Super 1000 title in men's singles. Kim Won-ho/Seo Seung-jae took men's doubles.

Jan 2026 India Open · Super 750

Lin Chun-yi surges past the field in New Delhi

Chinese Taipei's Lin Chun-yi won men's singles as An Se-young added another women's singles title. Liang Weikeng/Wang Chang triumphed in men's doubles.

3–8 Mar 2026 All England Open · Super 1000

Lin Chun-yi & Wang Zhiyi rule Birmingham

At the sport's most historic tournament, Lin Chun-yi beat Lakshya Sen for the men's title while Wang Zhiyi edged An Se-young in the women's final.

Jun 2026 Indonesia Open · Super 1000

Victor Lai stuns Jakarta; An Se-young reigns again

Victor Lai captured a breakout men's singles title in the Istora cauldron. Goh Sze Fei/Nur Izzuddin delighted the home crowd in men's doubles.

14–19 Jul 2026 Japan Open · Super 750

Fajar/Fikri seize Indonesia's first Super 750 of 2026

Fajar Alfian/Muhammad Shohibul Fikri beat Kim Won-ho/Seo Seung-jae in Tokyo. PV Sindhu won women's singles and France's Christo Popov took the men's crown.

21–26 Jul 2026 · Latest China Open · Super 1000

Chou Tien-chen, 36, becomes oldest Super 1000 champion

Chou Tien-chen beat Toma Junior Popov to make history in Changzhou. Akane Yamaguchi downed Chen Yufei for the women's title; Liu Shengshu/Tan Ning defended their women's doubles crown.

17–23 Aug 2026 · Upcoming World Championships · New Delhi

The world's best chase the rainbow jersey

The 2026 BWF World Championships head to New Delhi, where Akane Yamaguchi eyes back-to-back world titles. The battle for global supremacy begins 17 August.
